# Review A Universal Union

AUU needs independent readers before it needs agreement. The useful question is not “Do you support this?” but “What is unclear, unsafe, naive, contradictory, inaccessible, unsupported, or missing?”

No production system exists. These are concept, requirements, and research documents. A review does not endorse the project, and you do not need to read the entire repository to contribute.

A GitHub account is not required. If someone invited you by email, you can reply directly with your review. You can also share it in the [AUU Discord](https://discord.gg/5dsAEVt4Zy). GitHub is available as an optional public record for people who already use it.

## Choose a review route

| Route | Read or inspect | What to report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quick diagram check | Any diagram in the [diagram index](./docs/diagrams/README.md) | What you think it says, where the flow or text fails, and the device or accessibility settings used. |
| First-read comprehension | The [Reader's Map](./README.md), [Mission Statement](./v5-documents/0.mission-statement.md), and [functional model](./docs/diagrams/auu-functional-model.svg) | Explain AUU in your own words, then identify what remained unclear or appeared unsupported. |
| Focused document review | One document related to your experience or expertise | Identify a claim, requirement, omission, risk, contradiction, or research need and explain why it matters. |
| Whole-set reconstruction | The Reader's Map, numbered documents, glossary, and diagrams | Reconstruct the model without relying on the project's own summary, then identify mismatches, lost distinctions, and unsafe assumptions. |

Stopping early is useful evidence. If a route is too demanding, confusing, inaccessible, or difficult to navigate, report where and why.

## First-read questions

After reading, answer as many of these as you can without copying the project's wording:

1. What problem is AUU trying to address?
2. What is AUU, and what is it explicitly not?
3. Who may make a decision that binds someone else, and where does that authority come from?
4. What does the proposal refuse to guarantee at this stage?
5. How could a person challenge bad evidence, an unfair process, exceeded authority, or harmful results?
6. Which part was hardest to understand, easiest to misread, or most likely to fail in practice?
7. What important objection, person, circumstance, or alternative does the model appear to miss?

A misunderstanding is not automatically a reviewer failure. It may reveal that the project has not communicated a distinction clearly enough.

## Focused review areas

| If you can review… | Start with… | Look especially for… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rights, consent, ethics, or coercion | [Ethical Discussion](./v5-documents/1.ethical-discussion.md) | Conflicting rights, hidden power, unsafe assurances, absent people, and missing remedy. |
| Functions, architecture, privacy, or security | [Feature Sets](./v5-documents/2.feature-sets.md) | Dependency errors, authority confusion, threat boundaries, and requirements described as guarantees. |
| Research methods, evidence, or pilots | [R&D Strategy](./v5-documents/3.research-and-development-strategy.md) and [Research Index](./v5-documents/5.research.md) | Unfalsifiable claims, missing evidence, premature build steps, and weak stop conditions. |
| Institutions, representation, or governance | [Social Structures](./v5-documents/4.social-structures.md) | Manufactured legitimacy, capture, unclear standing, and authority without challenge or exit. |
| Accessibility, comprehension, or interface design | [User Experience Requirements](./v5-documents/6.user-experience.md) | Cognitive overload, exclusion, unsafe defaults, inaccessible processes, and misleading interface cues. |
| Existing tools or implementation reuse | [Tool Comparison](./v5-documents/7.tool-comparison.md) | Outdated comparisons, overstated maturity, missing tools, and integration assumptions. |
| Vocabulary and cross-document consistency | [Glossary](./v5-documents/GLOSSARY.md) | Terms that drift, collapse distinct relationships, or become impossible to apply. |

Credentials are helpful for some claims but are not required to notice a real problem. State the basis and limits of your review: professional knowledge, lived experience, a source, a test, a first reading, or a hypothesis that needs examination.

## Diagram checks

For a diagram review, include:

- the filename;
- device, browser, approximate screen or viewport, zoom, and any assistive technology;
- whether you viewed the default GitHub rendering, opened the SVG, or both;
- your one- or two-sentence interpretation before reading the related prose;
- text, arrows, grouping, order, or contrast that failed;
- any meaning that depended on color alone or changed at a smaller size; and
- any claim the diagram seemed to make more strongly than the documents do.

Do not force the comprehensive diagnostic into one screen. Test whether opening and zooming it is discoverable and whether a reader can orient themselves inside it.

## Submit feedback

No special format or GitHub account is required. Use as much of this short response template as is useful:

```text
Reviewed:
Review route:
What I understood:
What appeared unclear, unsafe, unsupported, missing, or especially strong:
Why it matters:
Basis and limits of my assessment:
Suggested response, if any:
Device or viewing conditions, if relevant:
```

If someone invited you by email, reply directly and include your notes in the message or an attachment. Otherwise, share them in the [AUU Discord](https://discord.gg/5dsAEVt4Zy). Rough notes, partial answers, and an explanation of where you stopped are all useful.

If you already have a GitHub account and want to create a durable public record yourself, use the optional [Review feedback form](https://github.com/A-Universal-Union/auu-project-docs/issues/new?template=review-feedback.yml).

Feedback sent by email or Discord is not automatically copied into the public repository. If a finding should become part of the durable project record, AUU will ask whether it may be quoted, summarized, and attributed before transferring it. You may ask to remain unnamed.

Do not include private information, identifying details, protected records, credentials, exploit instructions, or material you do not have permission to publish. A high-level description is enough when detail would create risk.

Review findings should be recorded as one or more of: comprehension failure, ambiguity, contradiction, accessibility barrier, unsupported claim, threat or abuse case, missing affected perspective, evidence need, design alternative, or confirmed strength. The project may clarify, research, test, change, defer, reject, or preserve an unresolved objection. A response is not proof that the concern has been resolved.
