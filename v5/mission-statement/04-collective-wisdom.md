## Learning across disagreement

Develop cooperation, reciprocal learning, institutional experimentation, and continued dissent. Bring in the current mission's policy experiment with its rights, authority, nonconsent, shared-obligation, and external-cost limits.

## Memory and peaceful correction

Connect evidence about consequences to civic memory, revision, and peaceful recourse. Account for failed learning, captured feedback, and the danger of permanent exposure; do not assume inevitable ideological convergence.
