## Human trust

Develop human dignity, agency, conscience, and relationships as the ethical foundation. Distinguish this commitment from the effectiveness of any proposed trust mechanism.

## People beyond the graph

Include isolated, vulnerable, dissenting, and unconnected people within the foundation. Their dignity and affected standing must not depend on finding witnesses.
