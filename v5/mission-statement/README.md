# A Universal Union: Mission Statement

## Thesis

**Status: Incomplete draft for author review.** Chapters I–II are drafted; Chapters III–VI remain outlines. The [current mission](../../v5-documents/0.mission-statement.md) remains canonical; this directory is the workspace for the expanded mission.

A Universal Union begins with faith in humanity. People deserve a meaningful part in shaping the world they have to live in: room to think, disagree, bring forward a concern, and find others prepared to act. Their dignity cannot depend on how useful, popular, or easy to manage they happen to be.

AUU proposes shared civic tools to help people turn those concerns into decisions, give people limited authority to carry them out, see what happened, and correct what went wrong. The ambition reaches further: to change conditions that reward manipulation and domination, and make cooperation and learning easier to carry forward across communities and generations. People must remain free to disagree, and people who cannot safely participate must still count.

Human dignity and relationships give the work its purpose. The designs proposed to serve them still need research, testing, and criticism. Conviction about people does not establish that a mechanism will work.
