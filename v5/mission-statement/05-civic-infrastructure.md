## From concern to accountable action

Explain originate → deliberate → authorize → delegate → act → record → challenge → repair → federate as possible civic functions, not a mandatory sequence. Preserve the distinction between authorization and real-world follow-through.

## Trust mechanisms and their limits

Explain witnessed relationships and bounded graphs as research candidates. Keep enrollment, connection limits, and recovery distinct; investigate inclusion, coercion, privacy, and appeal.

## Authority that can be challenged

Carry forward consent, affected standing, rights-floor governance, privacy for persons, reviewability for power, appeal, repair, and federation. Correct procedure does not establish a just outcome.
